Schedule
--------

This course is broken up into three units.

* Unit 1 (9/8–10/8): Logic

  * Week 1: Intro, Arguments

  * Week 2: Language, Truth tables

  * Week 3: Entailment, Semantic notions

  * Week 4: Quantifiers, Models

  * Week 5: Review, Exam

* Unit 2 (10/13–11/12): Statistics

  * Week 6: Intro, Visualizing data

  * Week 7: Averages, Range and quartiles

  * Week 8: Box and whisker plots, Standard deviation

  * Week 9: Normal distibutions, hypothesis testing

  * Week 10: Review, Exam

* Unit 3 (11/17–12/10): Modeling

  * Week 11: Intro, Functions

  * Week 12: Linear models, Exponential functions

  * Week 13: Exponential models, Logistic models

  * Week 14: Review, Exam
